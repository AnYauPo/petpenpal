We’ll take it down

If Pet Pen Pal shows an intimate image of you that was shared without your consent, we’ll remove it. This includes fake images, like ones made or changed with AI.

We remove it within 48 hours of a valid request, along with any identical copies we know about, as the US TAKE IT DOWN Act requires. We usually act much sooner.

You don’t need the app or an account to ask.

If you’re in danger right now, call 911 in the US, or your local emergency number.

Who can ask

The person shown in the image, or someone allowed to act for them, such as a parent or guardian of someone under 18, or a lawyer.

If you have the app

  • On the post, tap “···”, then Report, then “An intimate image of me”. The reported photo is normally hidden for everyone straight away. Abuse limits never stop your request reaching our team.
  • The form already knows which post it is. Say briefly what’s wrong, tick the box to confirm you didn’t consent, and type your full name as your signature.
  • We’ll reply to the email address on your account.

If you don’t have the app

Email petpenpal@anyaupo.com with the subject “Takedown request”, and include these four things:

  • What and where. A link to the post or pet if you have one, or the handle, the pet’s name, the caption and about when it was posted. Describe the image in a few words. Please don’t attach the image itself.
  • A statement that you didn’t consent. For example: “I believe in good faith that this intimate image of me was published without my consent.” Add anything that helps us understand, like that it was shared privately, taken without permission, or faked.
  • How to reach you. Your name and email address. If you’re asking for someone else, also say who they are and why you’re allowed to act for them.
  • Your signature. Typing your full name at the end is enough.

An email you can copy

To: petpenpal@anyaupo.com Subject: Takedown request What and where: [link, or handle, pet name, caption and date] The image shows: [a few words] I believe in good faith that this intimate image of me was published without my consent. [Anything else that helps] My name and email: [your name and email] (If acting for someone else) I am acting for: [their name, and how you’re allowed to] Signature: [type your full name]

What happens next

  • We confirm that we got your request.
  • We hide the image as soon as we find it, and remove it and its identical copies within 48 hours of a valid request. We find copies with a photo fingerprint.
  • If we can’t find the image from what you sent, or something’s missing, we ask you straight away.
  • We email you when it’s done.
  • The person who posted it is told that it broke our rules. We don’t tell them who asked. Their account may be paused or closed.

If the image shows someone under 18

We remove it, close the account that posted it, and report it to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC), as US law requires.

NCMEC’s free Take It Down service can help stop images of under-18s spreading on other sites too: https://takeitdown.ncmec.org. For adults, StopNCII.org does something similar: https://stopncii.org.

Please be honest

Only ask if you’re the person shown, or allowed to act for them, and you believe in good faith that the image was shared without consent. False requests hurt other people, and can break the law.

Other problems

  • Someone posted your photo, but it isn’t an intimate image? Report it with “This is my photo”, or see “If someone posts your photo” in our Terms.
  • Anything else unsafe? Report it in the app, or email petpenpal@anyaupo.com. We review reports within 48 hours.
