The short version

Pet Pen Pal is a place to celebrate pets. Keep it kind, safe and fair.

These rules apply to everything you put in Pet Pen Pal: photos, captions, handles and pet names. We have zero tolerance for objectionable content and for people who abuse others.

Pets only

  • Every post must be a photo of a pet or another animal. A dog posing with a cat is fine.
  • People can be in the background, but the pet is the star. Don’t post photos that are mostly about people.
  • No screenshots, ads, or images that are mostly text.

No nudity or sexual content

  • No nudity, sexual content or sexual poses, of anyone, ever. This includes drawings and images made or changed with AI.
  • Never post an intimate image of someone without their consent, including a fake one. We remove these within 48 hours of a valid request, and usually much sooner. See “Report an intimate image”.
  • Anything sexual involving someone under 18 is removed, the account is closed, and we report it to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC).

No cruelty, violence or gore

  • Don’t hurt, scare or tease an animal for a photo. No staged animal violence, blood or injuries. Photos from the vet are fine if they aren’t graphic.
  • No violence against people, and no threats.
  • Nothing that encourages dangerous stunts, self-harm or eating disorders.

If you’re struggling, or thinking about hurting yourself, please talk to someone you trust. In the US you can call or text 988, any time. Elsewhere, contact your local emergency number.

Be kind

  • No bullying or harassment, including making fun of someone’s pet.
  • No hate. Don’t target people for their race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, caste, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, illness or age.
  • No threats, and no encouraging others to pile on someone.

No spam or scams

  • No ads, selling, fake giveaways, or links that try to send people somewhere else.
  • Don’t sell, swap or give away animals through Pet Pen Pal.
  • Don’t post the same thing again and again. You can post up to 10 times a day.

Only photos you’re allowed to post

  • Post only photos you took, or ones the owner said you can use. Don’t pass off someone else’s pet as yours.
  • If someone posted your photo, report it with “This is my photo”.
  • Pet Pen Pal spots photos that another account already posted, and holds them for a person to review. Reposting your own photo is fine.
  • People who keep posting other people’s photos lose their accounts.

Keep personal details private

  • Don’t share anyone’s real name, address, school, phone number, email or exact location, including your own. That goes for captions, handles and pet names, and for things you can read in a photo, like a house number or a car’s number plate.
  • Don’t pretend to be someone else: another player, a famous person, a brand or the Pet Pen Pal team.

Play fair

  • Don’t use extra accounts, swap hearts in groups, use bots or changed apps, or use bugs to grow pets.
  • Hearts like these count for less, and we can take away the XP they earned and pause the accounts involved.

Nothing illegal

Don’t post or promote anything illegal, such as drugs, weapons sales or harming animals.

Reporting and blocking

  • To report a post, pet or handle, tap “···”, then Report. Only the Pet Pen Pal team sees reports, and the person isn’t told who reported them.
  • Once you report something, you won’t see it again. Two independent, established accounts can hide a reported photo or comment until review. An intimate image report hides only the reported photo straight away. Account reports go to our team; they never hide an account automatically.
  • Limits prevent repeated automatic hides. Every report still goes to our team. We review reports within 48 hours.
  • To block someone, tap “···”, then Block. You won’t see each other’s pets or posts, and neither of you can heart or follow the other’s pets. They aren’t told. You can unblock them in Settings › Blocked accounts.

What happens when rules are broken

  • The automatic safety check holds or stops posts that look unsafe, before anyone else sees them.
  • After a report, a person decides: keep the post, remove it, or pause the account.
  • When a post is removed, the XP it earned is taken back, and a level can drop.
  • Two removed posts pause posting until we review the account.
  • Serious breaks, like sexual content involving anyone under 18, intimate images shared without consent, or threats, pause the account straight away, and it can be closed for good.
  • If we remove your post, we tell you why, in Activity and by email. Players who reported it are told what we decided.

Appeals

Think we got it wrong? Reply to our email about the removal within 14 days, or write to petpenpal@anyaupo.com. A person will look again and tell you what they decided.

If the automatic check is holding your post, you can edit the caption or delete the post, or email us to ask a person to look.
