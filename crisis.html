What happens in chat

If your message sounds like you or someone else might be hurt or in danger, Chat sends a care reply written by the Pet Pen Pal team. It comes right away, even during quiet hours or after you have used your usual chat allowance. We cancel cheerful replies still waiting in that conversation and save no memory from your message.

Real help comes first

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services now. This chat is not emergency care, therapy or medical advice. No person monitors the conversation or automatically contacts emergency services for you. Do not wait for the pet to reply.

The care reply points you to a trusted adult or someone who can help. If we know your country or region from sign-up, it can give you the number for a crisis line and for emergency services. In the US it points to calling or texting 988, and 911 for immediate danger. Otherwise it suggests a local helpline and emergency number.

How we check messages

Our server rules check messages for signs of self-harm, suicide, abuse, bullying, exploitation or immediate danger. During the invite-only beta, adults approved by the Pet Pen Pal team who meet the age checks and agree to AI letters can get an additional Claude safety check. Everyone else keeps team-written replies. These checks can make mistakes and may miss a risk.

What we record

Each care reply adds one to an anonymous daily referral total. This counter contains no account, message ID or message text. Retries of the same message do not add another referral. These totals help us understand how often the protocol is used and can remain after account deletion.

Chat text follows the Privacy policy: detectable personal details are hidden before storage, typed chat is normally deleted after 30 days, and Delete memories and chat in Settings deletes chat and memories from the live database. Referrals do not create a separate copy of message text.

Children and AI disclosure

Pet Pen Pal accounts are for players aged 13 or older, or 16 in Europe and the UK. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn an account belongs to a child under 13, we close it and handle deletion under our Privacy policy and applicable law, including COPPA.

During the beta, AI letters are written with Claude from Anthropic only for adults who meet the age checks, have an invitation from the Pet Pen Pal team and turn the feature on with separate consent. Your pet is a game character. We explain how Claude is used when you turn on AI letters and in Settings > AI choices under AI letters. The pet does not claim to be a real animal or a person, encourage emotional reliance, pressure players to stay, or say it needs purchases. Signs that a player may be under 18 switch AI letters off. Only the team can clear that warning after review. A phone declaration cannot clear it or override a younger stored age.

Questions or concerns

For a concern about the protocol, contact petpenpal@anyaupo.com. Support is not an emergency service.
