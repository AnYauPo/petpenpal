The short version

  • Pet Pen Pal is for players aged 13 or older, or 16 in Europe and the UK. If we cannot confirm your region, the minimum is 16.
  • Your public pets, posts and comments can be seen by other players. Your email, age group and saved posts are private.
  • Location is optional. Earlier location choices may remain stored privately, but we do not receive coordinates.
  • AI looks send your original pet photo to an AI image service only after a separate consent step. Your kept AI look can become the public portrait. You can switch back to the original photo any time.
  • We keep basic usage analytics in our own Supabase database to understand returns and first steps. Signed-in activity is linked to your account.
  • Notifications are optional. Turn them off in Settings in Pet Pen Pal or in iOS Settings.
  • We do not sell your information, show ads or track you across other apps and websites.
  • You can delete your account in the app. Some safety, legal and provider records may remain as explained below.

Who we are

Pet Pen Pal is made by AnYauPo LLC in Washington State, USA. We decide how Pet Pen Pal uses your information. Data protection law calls us the controller.

For privacy questions or requests, email petpenpal@anyaupo.com.

Playing without an account

You can add a pet without an account. Its photos and game records stay on your phone. The pet check and cutout run on your phone.

Your phone keeps pet limits, slot locks and an age-refusal flag and the lowest age group answered on this device in secure storage. The first answer locks, so signing out, deleting an account or registering again does not allow an older birthday. These can survive deleting the app. We do not receive those local records unless a later account flow sends the allowed pet data.

We collect usage analytics under a random installation ID only while you are signed in; descriptions of signed-out app opens below refer to earlier versions of the app. This ID is saved in the app’s local storage. It is not an advertising ID. See “Usage analytics”.

Saving or sharing an image uses the destination you choose. That destination has its own privacy rules. If error reporting is configured, scrubbed JavaScript errors can also be sent while you are signed out. See “Error reports”.

Account and game information

  • Sign-in: your email address and account ID. Sign-in uses an email code.
  • Age check: your age group, 13 to 15, 16 to 17, or 18 and over. We also keep your country or region and the time of the check.
  • Profile: your handle, play style, tester status, privacy choices, guide progress and phone time zone. The time zone sets your game day.
  • Pets: original photos, cutouts, names, animal groups, personality, level and journals. We also generate and store hidden game data for possible future events, including numerical attributes, appearance categories, ability descriptions and identifying numbers. These are not shown in everyday play.
  • AI looks: your consent time and consent version, choices, generated looks and the confirmed appearance.
  • Community: post photos, captions, comments, replies, hearts, follows, saved posts and the status and timing of an automated first-post welcome.
  • Earlier social features: friend requests and their status and times, accepted friendships, reusable codes and link tokens, online presence, invitations and game results. These records can remain while the features are unavailable.
  • Earlier game sessions and future events: matching records, chosen actions, saved plans, results, placements, earned labels, reward history and regional totals. Existing records remain stored while events are unavailable.
  • Safety: reports, blocks, takedown requests, moderation decisions and photo fingerprints. A fingerprint is a short code used to spot copied photos.
  • Earlier location choices: your selected state and visibility setting, and any saved city, country, place IDs, choice times and location history. These remain private while location discovery and events are unavailable.
  • Support: messages you send us and the details needed to answer. Takedown requests ask for a signed name. Formal copyright notices also ask for a postal address and phone number.
  • Service records: sign-in and server request logs. Hosting providers can receive IP addresses and request details to operate and secure the service.
  • Notifications: if you enable server notifications, a push token for this installation and your notification preferences. We use them to send the notifications you choose.

Information we keep off our servers

  • Your birthday. If Apple cannot provide a usable age range, your phone uses the date you enter once to work out an age group. Only the group is saved or sent. The question starts blank, gives no age threshold and does not allow another answer.
  • Your location coordinates. The phone uses a location reading and geocoding to find place names. Apple or the device location service may process that reading. Pet Pen Pal receives only the place information described below.
  • Photo metadata such as GPS tags. The app prepares images before upload to remove this metadata. Details visible in a picture can still reveal a place or person.
  • Your phone contacts or your whole photo library. You choose which photos to use.
  • Advertising identifiers or information from other apps for tracking.

You do not need to give your real name to play. A name or contact detail you put in a public photo, caption or comment could be seen by others. Please keep personal details out of public content.

  • To check public content, handle reports, spot copied photos and enforce our rules.
  • To make AI looks when you unlock them and give consent.
  • To send sign-in codes and answer support and privacy requests. We do not send marketing emails.
  • To measure active players, return visits and first steps, and improve Pet Pen Pal.
  • To send optional reminders and notifications about your pets and activity.
  • To fix errors, protect the service and meet legal duties.

The public content safety check

Public photos and text go through safety checks. For photo and caption moderation, our server uses Claude from Anthropic. This can include original pet photos, posts and chosen AI looks. This check is separate from the service that makes AI looks.

A check can hold or reject content before publication. People review reports and appeals. If you think a decision is wrong, email petpenpal@anyaupo.com.

AI-assisted messages

Pet Pen Pal uses AI to help write some messages, including some Pet Pen Pal Crew comments. To write a welcome on a first post, we send Claude from Anthropic the post caption, pet name and animal kind. We do not send the photo, account handle, email or age for this writing step. A caption can still contain personal information you put in it.

An automated welcome comes only from the labelled Pet Pen Pal Crew account. It is checked before posting and follows the same comment rules as player comments. It does not automatically continue a conversation. The generated text is also sent to Anthropic for a safety check. Anthropic processes these text details under its service terms and retention settings.

Chatting with your pet

You can type messages to your pet, and it writes back. Your pet is a game character, not your real pet or a person. During the beta, AI-written letters are invite-only for adults approved by the Pet Pen Pal team who separately agree to chat sharing with Anthropic (Claude). Everyone else keeps prepared, automatic team-written replies. No person replies. Before the first AI letter, an unticked choice explains the service, data and retention. We record the consent version and time. Accepting the terms does not give this consent. Declining keeps team-written letters without repeated requests. Settings > Chat memory > AI letters lets you withdraw at any time. Withdrawal stops future chat sharing and changes queued AI letters to team-written ones. It cannot undo processing already completed. Removing a beta invitation also stops new AI letters and replaces queued AI letters with team text, while keeping the consent record.

Approved testers must also meet the existing age checks. The device can report a confirmed adult age group; this app-reported signal is not independent proof of age. Through 31 December 2026 only, an adult age group from the birthday entered at sign-up also meets the age check if the device locale region saved at sign-up is US. This uses no location reading. An unknown or other region keeps the confirmed-only rule. At 1 January 2027, 00:00 UTC, the typed-birthday route ends automatically. An under-18 birthday, any under-18 device signal or an age flag always keeps team-written letters. A later older signal never overrides a younger recorded age group. We privately keep each approved account ID, who added it, when and a short approval note.

We send your message after hiding personal details we can detect, your pet's name and kind, where it is and what it is doing, its outfit and travel route, the name it calls you, up to six recent messages from this chat, and sometimes one thing it remembers. We send your message and its reply for safety checks too. Automatic hiding can miss things, so keep personal details private.

Anthropic does not train its models on our API messages by default. It normally deletes API inputs and outputs within 30 days. Safety or policy enforcement, legal duties or a different agreed retention arrangement can require longer retention.

Claude checks incoming messages for signs that the player may be under 18, such as their own stated age or school year. Local server rules do this too, before AI sharing where possible. School alone and adults talking about their children are not age cues. A cue flags the account and switches future letters to team-written replies. Only the Pet Pen Pal team can clear this flag after review. A phone declaration cannot clear it. Clearing a flag cannot override a recorded under-18 age group or device signal. The flag and age group are private.

Players who are ineligible, decline, withdraw or have used the daily AI allowance get team-written replies. Their new typed messages are not sent to an outside AI service. Adults also get team-written replies when the service is unavailable or safety checks reject a reply. Chat has a separate allowance of 30 AI letters per account per UTC day, independent of photo checks. We keep account-linked counts and token usage to enforce the allowance and estimate cost, without copying chat text into those records.

For approved adult testers who meet the age checks and have current chat consent, a reply to one of the suggested lines can be rewritten by Claude from Anthropic. It receives the pet’s name and kind, the city, spot and outfit, the name the pet calls you, the suggested line you chose and the time of day. For a clue reply, it also receives the clue text. This rewrite never receives your typed messages.

We keep your typed messages and your pet’s chat replies with your account for 30 days so the conversation stays in the app, then delete them. Before a message is saved or used to write a reply, we hide full names, schools, addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, social media handles and links that we can detect. This is automatic and can miss things, so it is best not to type them. The pet never asks for them or for photos of you. A reply is written from your latest messages in this chat session only, or the last day for confirmed adults.

Your pet quietly remembers a few things from chat and brings them up later. These are short notes from a fixed list, not copies of your messages, and there is no list to read. Anyone can have game memories, like a city you love or an outfit you like. If you are 18 or over and your phone’s age check confirms it, your pet can also remember small things about your day, like an exam, a hobby, a friend’s first name or a rough day. Everyone else, including players whose age is not confirmed, keeps only game memories. If your age stops being confirmed, those day memories are deleted straight away. A later age check from your phone can move your account to a younger age group, which turns on the teen settings, but never to an older one.

Every message is checked in both directions, yours and the pet’s. The pet stays on its trips, you, your day and the game. It never claims to be a person and avoids romance. Every player gets the same chat protections, whatever their age, including a visible label explaining who writes the replies and an hourly reminder during continuous foreground chat with AI letters on. The pet never claims to be alive or the real animal, guilts you for leaving, says it needs purchases, or encourages emotional reliance. If a message sounds like someone could be hurt or in danger, including bullying, abuse, or someone asking for photos, a meeting or secrets, the reply is written by our team, not by AI. It points to a trusted adult and, where we know your country from sign-up, a helpline there, like calling or texting 988 in the US. The published crisis protocol explains this at https://petpenpal.anyaupo.com/crisis.html. We count care referrals in anonymous daily totals with no account, message ID or message text. These totals can remain after account deletion. Chat is not emergency care or a service monitored by a person.

About once a day your pet may write first. A new pet’s first typed letter and first suggested line each get a reply in about a minute, at any hour. We keep two first-reply flags until the pet or account is deleted; Forget everything does not reset them. After these introductions, confirmed adults have no overnight reply pause. For players under 18 and anyone not confirmed adult, new messages sent from 10 pm to 7 am wait until 8 am for a reply. These hours follow your saved local time zone, wherever your pet is. A reply already promised keeps its time. Flights can take longer, and safety replies do not wait. With Messages from your pet turned on in Notifications, replies and pet messages can send an alert from 9 am to 8 pm, narrowed by your quiet hours. Replies still arrive in Chat outside alert hours; an unread reply’s alert can wait until the alert window opens.

Forget everything, in Settings under Chat memory, deletes what your pets remember, your typed messages, their chat replies and the name they call you from our live database straight away. AI-written replies to suggested lines go back to our team’s words, and a message you send while it runs is not saved. Trips, postcards, quests and suggested-line taps stay. Deleting your account deletes your chat and memories too. Backups and provider logs follow “How long information stays”.

AI looks and photo sharing

AI looks are optional. Before your account first sends a photo for AI looks, we ask for separate consent. We ask again before your next AI look if the service or sharing terms change. Players aged 13 to 17 give their own consent on the same screen as adults. There is no separate parent consent step in the app.

Pet Pen Pal's own computer makes AI looks and AI photos. Claude from Anthropic checks their results for safety. We do not send your account ID, handle, your location, captions, friends, Gems or purchases. Details visible in a photo can still identify someone.

For AI looks, the picture maker gets your original photo and style instructions to make three choices. Claude gets the results for safety checks. For AI photos, Claude also checks the saved portrait and reference pictures for outfit quality.

Each redo also uses the original photo. We record your consent time, the version of the consent text and your most recent AI looks withdrawal. In this test build, AI looks use Qwen Image Edit on the picture maker. Outfit and trip photos use Flux Klein there. Finishing accessory photos uses Qwen Image Edit there. These models run on that computer; Claude safety and outfit checks go to Anthropic.

The picture maker can keep photos you send, work records and results. We have not confirmed when it deletes them, so we cannot promise a deletion time. Anthropic keeps checks under its service terms and retention settings.

AI looks and AI photos have separate consent. We ask again if the service or sharing terms change.

At launch, the service will use a third-party AI image provider. That provider has not been chosen. The consent screen must identify the active service before you send a photo. A provider change or a material change to this sharing needs a new consent version.

You preview the looks and choose one. The look you keep becomes public like the pet’s photo. You can switch back to the original photo any time. The original photo stays saved separately on the pet’s page. Unchosen previews are private.

Pet Pen Pal keeps your original and saved results until removed. AI looks waiting for your choice stay private. Unused previews after a choice and abandoned failed previews are scheduled for removal after seven days. Removal can need retries.

Open Settings > AI choices, or the AI photos row in Wardrobe, and choose Turn off AI photos. This stops new AI photos and unfinished work. AI looks have separate consent. Choose Turn off AI looks in AI choices to stop future AI looks and unfinished looks. Saved looks and previews follow the same retention rules. Withdrawal cannot recall work already sent, completed work or outside copies. You can switch back to the original photo any time. Delete your pet or account to start removing saved photos. Outside copies may remain.

Usage analytics

Pet Pen Pal keeps first-party usage events in its own Supabase database. We do not use a third-party product analytics service, advertising identifiers or cross-app tracking.

Events cover daily app opens, completed sign-up, the first pet imported or created, posts created, AI look confirmations and finished tutorial steps. Earlier and event-only game sessions can also keep queue, timing, connection, chosen-action and result records. We use these to count active players, return visits and first steps, and understand reliability.

Signed-in events carry your account ID. App opens also carry a random installation ID, the app version, platform and local date. Signed-out app opens carry that installation ID with no account ID. Server events use the time and game records already on our server. We use existing signed-in installation IDs to spot connected reports and reduce false automatic hides.

Event properties use a fixed list: dates, whether a pet was imported or made on the server, reward status, an appearance unlock or tutorial step, and session timings and results. Analytics contain no photos, captions, handles, pet names, free text, screen names or location.

We also measure how long a first post waits for its first heart or comment. We keep the first-post owner, post ID and time, first response times, and whether responses came from Pet Pen Pal Crew. This measures first-post responses without storing captions or comment text in analytics.

You can ask us to delete account-linked analytics. Deleting an account removes its linked events. Earlier signed-out app opens cannot reliably be linked back to an account and may remain.

Notifications

Notifications can tell you that your pet wrote to you, about comments and followers, and when a new AI look is ready. Messages from your pet are on by default; you can turn them off in Settings › Notifications. All alerts require phone permission and respect your system notification settings, including Do Not Disturb and Focus. They use the standard interruption level and are grouped by pet.

If you enable server notifications, we store a push token and your notification preferences to deliver them. Expo’s push service and Apple’s notification service process the token and notification payload. Notifications can show a pet name and activity type on your lock screen.

Players under 18 have quiet hours from 9 pm to 8 am in their saved local time zone. This stays on. Pet messages use the narrower window of 9 am to 8 pm for everyone, even when quiet hours are off.

Turn off notifications or individual types in Settings in Pet Pen Pal. You can also turn off alerts in phone settings. Notifications are not required to play and are not used for advertising or tracking.

Error reports

Sentry error reporting is off until Pet Pen Pal configures it. When enabled, it receives scrubbed JavaScript error reports to help us fix bugs. It does not capture native crashes.

Reports exclude photos, image paths and links, handles, names, captions, email addresses, account IDs and locations. Request bodies, sign-in headers and tokens are excluded too. We do not attach your account to an error report.

Screen recordings, screenshots, breadcrumbs, application logs, tracing, profiling and automatic session collection are disabled. This reporting is not used for ads or tracking.

What other people can see

  • Other players can see your handle, visible pet profiles, original profile photos, chosen AI looks, posts, comments and public counts.
  • Shared images can be seen by anyone you send them to. Other people can copy or screenshot public content.
  • Location discovery is unavailable in this build. Earlier location choices stay private.
  • Your login email, age group, account region, saved home city, time zone, saved posts, blocks and reports are not public.
  • Earlier friend requests, friendships, invitations and shared game results remain visible to their participants where those features are available. These are not public lists.
  • A pet’s follower list is available only to its owner. The follower count is public. Reports do not identify the reporter to the reported player. Blocks do not send a notice.

The Pet Pen Pal team and its service providers can access information needed to run the service, handle support and keep it safe.

Service providers

  • Supabase hosts our database, sign-in, photo storage and server functions in the United States.

Invite previews contain no pet photo or handle. The invite token stays in the link fragment, which is not sent to the website host. The host may receive ordinary web request logs.

We use providers to operate Pet Pen Pal. We do not give them information for targeted advertising. Their processing terms and retention settings also apply to the information they handle for us.

Other sharing

If Pet Pen Pal or AnYauPo LLC changes ownership, information may pass to the new owner. This policy continues to apply unless it is changed with the notice described below.

How long information stays

Account and game information normally stays while your account exists. Deleting a post, pet or account starts removal of its associated information and stored media. Media removal may need a retry if storage is unavailable.

Pending AI look previews stay private so you can return to a choice. Unselected previews after confirmation and abandoned failed previews are scheduled for cleanup after seven days. Consent and generation records remain while needed to run the feature and handle requests.

Earlier pending friend requests expire after seven days and short-lived invitations after 30 seconds. Attempt records can remain to enforce limits. Codes and links stay valid until reset. Friendships, presence settings, invitation records and shared game results normally stay while the account exists.

Safety reports, moderation decisions, photo fingerprints and support correspondence may remain while needed for appeals, repeat abuse, legal claims or legal duties. We do not use these records for advertising.

Hosting, email, safety-check and AI providers can retain service logs or backup copies under their retention settings and legal duties. Deletion from the live app does not mean every provider backup is erased at once.

Email petpenpal@anyaupo.com to ask about retention or request deletion of a specific record.

Deleting an account

Use Settings › Delete account. The app asks you to confirm twice and offers to save your pet portraits and postcards first.

Deletion removes your sign-in account and associated profile, pets, posts, comments, original and generated photos, hearts, follows, saves, blocks, friendships and requests, codes and link tokens, presence, invitations, game records, first-post response measurements, account-linked analytics events, push tokens and notification preferences. Earlier signed-out app opens remain as explained in “Usage analytics”. Records needed for safety or law, provider logs and backups may remain as described above.

XP already credited to other pets and aggregate game totals may remain. Copies shared outside Pet Pen Pal, such as screenshots, are outside our control.

Signing out keeps the account on our servers and clears its local account data from the phone. It is not account deletion.

If you cannot get into the app, email petpenpal@anyaupo.com from your login email to request deletion. We may need to check that the account is yours.

Teens and children

Players must be at least 13, or 16 in Europe and the UK. If we cannot confirm your region, the minimum is 16. Children below the applicable minimum cannot join. If we learn that an account belongs to a child under 13, we close it and remove its information, subject to any legal duties. A parent or guardian can contact petpenpal@anyaupo.com.

Teen chat and comment protections apply to players under 18 and players whose age is not confirmed. Location discovery and events are unavailable in this build.

If either a commenter or a post owner is under 18, the commenter must follow that pet before commenting. Owners can comment on their own posts. Comments are public, not private messages. Post owners can turn comments off.

Follower lists are private for all ages. There are no private messages between players. Public pet photos, posts and comments may still reveal personal details, so keep those details out.

Teens give their own consent before sharing photos for AI looks. The app does not ask a parent to consent for them.

Your choices and rights

You can ask for a copy of your information, a correction, deletion, or a restriction on how we use it. Depending on where you live, you can also object to processing or ask for a portable copy.

Email petpenpal@anyaupo.com. We may verify your identity and will respond within the time required by law. We aim to answer within 30 days. Using your rights does not change how we treat you in the game.

In the app, you can change your handle and privacy settings and delete your posts, comments, pets or account. Email us to remove earlier location choices that have no control in this build.

Europe, the UK and international transfers

For core account and game features, we use information needed to provide the service described in the Terms. For safety and security, we rely on our legitimate interests, balanced against your rights. We also process information to meet legal duties.

Optional location readings, AI looks and AI photos depend on your consent. You can stop new location readings in your phone settings. In Settings > AI choices, choose Turn off AI photos or Turn off AI looks to stop future work of that kind. The AI photos row in Wardrobe also opens AI choices. Withdrawal does not change whether earlier processing was lawful.

Supabase stores Pet Pen Pal information in the United States. Other providers may process information in other countries. Where data protection law requires safeguards for a transfer, we must arrange them before the transfer. You can ask us about those safeguards.

You can complain to your local data protection authority if you think we have handled information unfairly.

US state privacy rights

We do not sell personal information or share it for targeted advertising. We offer the requests above to everyone. Additional rights may apply under the law where you live.

Security and changes

We use private photo storage, expiring image links and access controls. These protections do not make public content private or prevent screenshots. No service can promise perfect security.

If this policy changes, we update the date above. We will give notice before an important change takes effect. A material change to photo sharing for AI looks requires renewed consent before a new submission.
